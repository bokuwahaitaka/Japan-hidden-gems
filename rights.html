Rights and takedown

JHG does not host full songs, music videos or full lyrics. It links to or embeds official third-party services where available.

Request a correction or removal

If you are an artist, label, publisher or other rights holder and believe a listing, image, preview, link or user contribution should be corrected or removed, send a request through the JHG feedback box. Choose “Content” and include the song, the affected material, your relationship to it and the requested action.

Potentially infringing material may be hidden while a request is reviewed. Please do not include passwords, identity documents or other sensitive information in the feedback box.
