JAPAN HIDDEN GEMS

Community guidelines

Help listeners discover Japanese music with accurate, respectful and useful contributions.

Contribute responsibly

  • Use official song and artist information where possible.
  • Write original cultural notes and recommendations.
  • Do not paste full lyrics, copyrighted articles or unauthorized audio.
  • Do not impersonate artists, labels or other users.
  • Do not manipulate rankings, create spam accounts or coordinate fake votes.
  • Respect people across languages, regions and musical tastes.

Corrections and moderation

Translations and metadata suggestions may be reviewed, merged or rejected. Abusive, misleading or rights-infringing material may be removed. Use the report and suggestion tools when something appears inaccurate.
