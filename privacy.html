JAPAN HIDDEN GEMS

Privacy

JHG uses an anonymous or signed-in Supabase account to save preferences and prevent duplicate responses. We collect a broad region, an age band, language, music preferences and voluntary listening activity. We do not ask for an exact age, legal name or precise location.

How data is used

Responses are used for recommendations, privacy-safe aggregate rankings and service improvement. Public metrics do not expose individual listening histories. Optional public profiles only show information the user chooses to publish.

Your choices

You may use the core service anonymously, change notification settings, report inaccurate data and request account deletion. Reference starter scores are separated from real listener responses.

Third-party services

Official YouTube, Spotify and Apple Music links may be opened. Their own privacy terms apply after leaving JHG.
