Read the room

On trains, keep calls and loud conversation to a minimum. At temples, homes and some restaurants, notice footwear cues before stepping inside.

Think region, not weather

Japan stretches far north–south. Hokkaido and Okinawa can feel like different countries in the same week; plan layers and check local forecasts.
