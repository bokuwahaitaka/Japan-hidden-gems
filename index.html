METHOD

How the score works

Hidden Gem Score = Japan Recommendation × Overseas Rating × (1 − Overseas Awareness)

The purpose is not to decide which song is “objectively best.” It is to identify a discovery gap: music strongly recommended in Japan, rarely known abroad, but positively received after listening.

WHY THIS EXISTS

Not another popularity chart.

Popularity algorithms tend to reinforce what people already know. Japan Hidden Gems is designed to ask a different question: Which Japanese songs deserve a first listen outside Japan?

ABOUT THE PROJECT

Discovery before popularity.

Japanese listeners nominate songs. Overseas listeners report whether they already knew them, then rate them after listening. The gap between domestic recommendation and overseas awareness helps surface overlooked music.
